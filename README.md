# Folliq

Red light scalp care for women with thinning hair.

**Website:** [myfolliq.com](https://myfolliq.com)

## What we make

**Red Light Scalp Massager**: a 660nm LED array combined with gentle massage, used for a six-minute session at home. Approx. 35 mW/cm2 at scalp contact, 12 J/cm2 per session.

**Rosemary Hair Growth Serum**, **Scalp Nourishing Oil** and **Scalp Derma Roller** complete the routine.

## A note on the evidence

The published red light hair studies we reference tested 655nm laser devices, not our 660nm LEDs. We say so on our product page, we have not run a clinical trial of our own, and we don't claim to cure or treat hair loss.
